Wakehug Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Provided by ZenBa · zenbainc@gmail.com

The short version

  • Wakehug is an AI voice assistant that calls you at times you choose.
  • During a live call, your voice streams to Google so the assistant can hear and answer you. Wakehug's server does not keep the audio or what you said.
  • Call recordings (on by default) are saved with a transcript only on your iPhone, so you can listen back. They are never uploaded. Turn recording off in Settings › Calls › Record calls, and delete any recording at any time.
  • Your call history, notes, memories and followed topics are stored on your iPhone, and copied to your own iCloud account so they follow you to a new iPhone.
  • There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We never sell or share your personal information, and Wakehug never sends messages for you.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifiers, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Your first name, if you type it, is stored on your iPhone and sent with each call (like your calendar) so the assistant can greet you; it isn't kept after the call.

Service providers

  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI, Cloud Run, Firestore, Text-to-Speech) processes call audio and text to provide the service. Under Google Cloud's terms, customer data is not used to train Google's models.
  • Apple (alarms, App Attest, Apple Weather, Maps, iCloud, in-app purchases). Apple Weather and Maps receive an approximate location only.

Your choices

  • Turn calendar, reminders, or weather and commute on or off in Settings › Your day.
  • Turn call recording off in Settings › Calls › Record calls.
  • Delete history, recordings, notes and memories in Settings › Privacy › Delete everything, or single memories in Settings › What Wakehug remembers.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. Data in your iCloud account can be removed in your iPhone's Settings › Apple ID › iCloud.
  • To ask about or delete server-side usage records, email zenbainc@gmail.com.

Children

Wakehug is intended for adults (18 and over), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Requests to Wakehug's server must come from a genuine copy of the app (Apple App Attest), travel over encrypted connections, and are rate-limited. The server keeps no audio or call content.

Changes

If what we share changes, the app will ask you to agree again before your next call, and this page will show the new effective date.
