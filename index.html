Wakehug Support

An AI assistant that calls you, in English or 中文.

Wakehug rings your iPhone like an alarm at the times you choose: a morning briefing, reminders, a call before meetings and an evening wind-down. Answer and talk: it knows your calendar and reminders, and can set calls, add reminders and events, save notes and draft messages for you.

Common questions

  • A call didn't ring. Open Settings › Alarms › Wakehug on your iPhone and make sure alarms are allowed. In Wakehug, Settings › Alarm check rings a test call. Keep a backup alarm for mornings that really matter.
  • The assistant can't hear me. Allow the microphone in iPhone Settings › Wakehug, and unlock your iPhone after answering from the lock screen.
  • It doesn't know my schedule. In Wakehug, Settings › Your day: turn on Calendar and Reminders. If iPhone says no, tap the hint to open iPhone Settings and allow access.
  • Recordings. Live calls are recorded only on your iPhone. Play or delete them from Recent calls; turn recording off in Settings › Calls › Record calls.
  • Plans and minutes. Free includes 10 live minutes a month in the iPhone voice. Plus and Pro add natural voices and more minutes. Manage or cancel in your iPhone's Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions; Wakehug › Settings › Plans › Restore purchases restores them on a new iPhone.
  • Deleting your data. Settings › Privacy › Delete everything removes history, recordings, notes and memories from your iPhone.

Emergencies

Wakehug is not an emergency service and cannot call anyone. In an emergency call 911. If you are thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
