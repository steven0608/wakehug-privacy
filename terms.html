1. What Wakehug is

Wakehug is an AI voice assistant that rings your iPhone at times you choose, talks with you, and can take actions you ask for (set Wakehug calls, add reminders and calendar events, save notes, draft messages for you to review). It is software, not a person, and not a telephone service.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older to use Wakehug.

3. No guarantee that a call rings

Calls depend on your iPhone, iOS, its settings and permissions, battery, connectivity and Apple's alarm system. We do not guarantee that any call will ring or connect. Don't rely on Wakehug as your only alarm for anything important; keep a backup alarm.

4. AI can be wrong

Answers and summaries are generated by AI and may be inaccurate or incomplete, including information about your calendar, the news, markets or sports. Check anything important. Wakehug does not provide medical, legal, financial or investment advice, and nothing it says is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

5. Emergencies

Wakehug is not an emergency service and cannot call anyone for you. In an emergency, call 911. If you are thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

6. Your actions and messages

Wakehug drafts messages but never sends them; you are responsible for anything you send. Actions it takes on your iPhone (calls, reminders, events) are shown with Undo; check them.

7. Recordings

Wakehug records your live calls with the assistant on your iPhone only, and you can turn this off. The only other party is the AI assistant. If someone else is talking near your iPhone during a call, their voice may be recorded too; you are responsible for telling them.

8. Acceptable use

Don't use Wakehug to harass, threaten, deceive or impersonate anyone, for anything illegal, or to try to break, overload or reverse-engineer the service. The "Step away" feature uses made-up callers for your own use; don't use it to deceive others in ways that cause harm.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. A subscription renews automatically at the same price and period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Where a free trial is offered, it is charged at the end of the trial unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your iPhone's Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.
  • When a month's minutes run out, calls still ring and speak in the iPhone voice.
